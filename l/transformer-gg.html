Transformer.gg

Transformer.gg is a marketplace for sponsored posts on X. Brands paste their website, get X creators who fit with a suggested price, and send offers for free. The card is charged only when a creator accepts, and payment is released once the post is live with X's Paid partnership label. Every offer has a tracked link. Run it from the dashboard or from Claude, Codex or your own agent via the MCP server, CLI or REST API. Free to join; 20% fee on paid offers.
