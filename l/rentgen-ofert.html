Rentgen Ofert

Every house and flat sale listing in the Polish Slaskie voivodeship, aggregated twice daily from five portals (Otodom, OLX, Gratka, Morizon, n-online), de-duplicated, with price history and cross-referenced notarial-deed (RCN) transaction prices. Published as a schema.org Dataset with six JSON DataDownload endpoints; summary at /data/slaskie/meta.json.
