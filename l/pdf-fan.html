PDF Fan

PDF Fan is a browser-based PDF toolkit with machine-readable tool guides and limits at /llms.txt. The Spanish insert-pages tool adds blank sheets or pages from a second PDF at a chosen position. It is free and requires no PDF Fan account. Original PDFs are processed in the browser. The generated PDF may be uploaded as a temporary copy for the download link.
