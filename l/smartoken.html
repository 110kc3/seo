SmarToken

Developers integrating several model providers often manage separate APIs and billing. SmarToken is an independent OpenAI-compatible gateway for supported Chinese text models, with a prepaid USD wallet, per-key budgets and usage logs. Eligible accounts may receive a one-time $1 trial if approved; it expires 30 days after grant. Conversations and responses are retained until deleted and may be accessed by authorized administrators.
