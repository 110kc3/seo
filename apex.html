The rail underneath

Every paid endpoint here settles the same way: HTTP 402 with machine-readable terms, an EIP-3009 authorization signed by the caller, settlement in USDC on Base through the Coinbase CDP facilitator. No account, no dashboard, no human step — an agent that can sign a transaction can buy from any of it in one session.

That rail is proven rather than intended: it has taken real settlements on mainnet, refuses replays, and publishes its terms at /api/x402/info so a caller can read the price without provoking a 402.

What is honest about the state of this

One service is live. The domain is an umbrella because more are intended, not because more exist — additional services get their own subdomain as they ship, and this page will list them when they do rather than before.

The other honest number: agents are arriving — 12.36% of requests to the live service are AI agents and the share is rising — and not one of them has ever paid for anything. That finding, with the measurements behind it, is published in full at the state of the agent web. It is the most useful thing here and it is free.
