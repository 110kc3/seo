Before you connect

Health checks cover a rotating sample. Failed observations carry their own date; no recorded failure does not mean a successful check. Even an HTTP response does not prove that authentication, payment or a tool operation will succeed.

  1. Check the result’s HTTP method and chain against your x402 client.
  2. Obtain the provider’s request schema and current payment terms. The endpoint URL alone is not a complete request example.
  3. Review the asset, recipient and amount before authorizing payment. This directory has not verified the paid result.
